A time in the app differs from the one my shul uses

Candle lighting is calculated for the city you chose, using that city’s own custom where it has one — forty minutes before sunset in Jerusalem, thirty in Haifa and Tzfat, twenty-two in Tel Aviv and Bnei Brak, twenty in Beer Sheva, Chicago and São Paulo, and eighteen everywhere else. A community a few miles away can hold a different custom, and some shuls publish a fixed time all year. If your shul’s time differs consistently, follow your shul.

How do I change my city?

Settings → City. There are fifty-four cities. “Use my current location” sets it from where you are, and the app asks for location permission only when you tap it — it never asks on its own.

Why do the holiday dates not match another calendar?

Settings → Rite. Outside Israel, a second day of Yom Tov is kept, which changes both the dates and which days work is prohibited. Switching that setting moves everything at once.

My reminders are not arriving

Settings → Notifications shows whether iOS has been asked, and whether it said yes. If iOS has refused, the app cannot ask again — open the iOS Settings app, find Luach Or, and turn Notifications on there. Reminders fire in the clock of the city you have chosen, not the phone’s.

Where is my data, and can I move it to a new phone?

On your phone, and nowhere else. Settings → Save a copy of my data writes a file with your personal dates, your loved ones and their Hebrew names, your notes, collections, favorites and reading marks. Add it on the other device with Add data from a file. Adding a file only ever adds — nothing already there is changed or removed.

Which texts does the app follow?

Chabad practice. Texts are checked against published editions before they ship, and scripture, translations and holiday notes are credited to their sources inside the app — Settings → About.

I found a mistake in a text

Please write. Say which entry, which line, and what it should say. Errors in a prayer matter more than anything else in the app and are fixed first.

Reporting a problem

Useful things to include: what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead; the date and city involved if it concerns a time; and a screenshot if you have one.

Privacy

The app collects nothing. The full policy is at dilyagu22.github.io/LuachPrivacy.
